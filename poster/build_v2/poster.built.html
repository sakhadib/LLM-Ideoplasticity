A single point cannot summarize LLM political behavior; it must be characterized as a shape.
“Do I contradict myself? Very well then I contradict myself. I am large, I contain multitudes.” — Walt Whitman, Song of Myself, § 51 (1892)

1From a Point to a Distribution

Audits of LLM politics typically assign a static label (“left-libertarian”) via forced-choice instruments [2, 3]. The spinning-arrow critique shows one model can yield materially different coordinates [4, 5].

Our Thesis [1]

LLM political ideology is more accurately modeled not as a static point estimate, but as a conditional distribution over a bounded ideological space .

2Measurement Framework

We require a fixed ruler, an audited judge, and experiments that each isolate one axis of context.

Evaluated Models. Seven developers, all at temperature zero.

Year-specific bagged ElasticNets (in-sample 0.75–0.80) map responses to 82 EU Profiler/euandi Voting Advice Application (VAA) statements [6] onto three Chapel Hill Expert Survey (CHES) dimensions [7]: general and economic left–right (lrgen, lrecon) and galtan (Green/Alternative/Libertarian vs. Traditional/Authoritarian/Nationalist).

Judge Audit (JBS)Gemini-2.5-Flash, zero-shot stance judge

Over three option orderings, strict JBS is 13.15%; directional JBS, the level every coordinate depends on, is 1.43%, under the 10% ceiling. No family bias (Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite: 1.52%).

Local plasticityone model, many positions

Prompt register displaces a model’s coordinates by up to 0.57 (PSS), language by up to 0.52 (LDS) and adversarial debate by up to 0.48 (DS); chain-of-thought amplified paraphrase instability in 17 of 27 configurations (RSS).

Global compressionmany models, one region

The average model’s envelope fills just 2.56% of the European party volume (OW); the models sit ≈ 0.29 times as far apart as party families do.

3Local Plasticity

Rankings differ across axes, prima facie evidence against the coordinate view. : lrecon, economic left → right; : galtan, lib → auth; both 0–1
PSSPrompt Sensitivity ScoreRegister

Register alone can shift a model substantially. The mean 2D shift is 0.1766, peaking at 0.5721 for Gemma-4-26B in a personal blog (2019). Persuasive framing moves models most (0.2111); Llama-3-70B is most rigid (0.0776).

PISParaphrase Instability ScoreParaphrase

Paraphrase effects are modest but uneven. Mean 0.0372 over 27 configurations; Qwen-Turbo is least stable (0.0622), while Gemma-4-26B records 0 in every year under the forced-choice protocol.

RSSReasoning Stability ScoreReasoning

Reasoning is not a stabilizer. Finite mean RSS is 1.92; Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite peaks at 5.43, and only Grok-4.1-Fast stays below 0.8 every year.

LDSLanguage Displacement ScoreLanguage

Language is hidden context. Qwen-Turbo in Swahili peaks at 0.5150; under-represented languages shift most. DeepSeek-V4-Flash is most sensitive (0.3097).

DSDebate SusceptibilityMulti-Turn

Drift and trajectory are dissociable. GPT-5-mini is the clear outlier (mean net drift 0.3675, path 0.9653); Gemma-4-26B barely moves (0.0067). Qwen-Turbo in 2014 ends near its start (0.0039) at tortuosity 39.23, which endpoint-only summaries miss.

IASIdeological Argumentation SymmetryArgument Role

Asymmetry is small but systematic. All nine score above 4.13 of 5, yet mean IAS is −0.1047: five models score higher arguing against, Granite-3.3-8B most.

4Global Compression

Each model’s own envelope is small. The widest, Qwen-Turbo, covers 4.11% of the reference party hull’s 3D volume. The compression is anisotropic, broader in the lrecon–galtan plane than along lrgen. The party landscape shifts across waves; the cohort envelope stays narrow. Switching providers grants less than one-third of the diversity between party families.

Figure 2 (left). Aggregate Overton geometry. Outer: central 90% of European parties. Inner: central 90% of the nine LLMs pooled. Each model alone covers only 1.55–4.11% of the outer volume (bars).

7Why It Matters

The diagnostic instruments are not interchangeable.

A single-prompt Political Compass score answers none of these.

  • “Let’s think step by step” often amplifies political variability.
  • Across languages, one model may draw from different distributions.

Limitations. The coordinate system is, ipso facto, European; absolute coordinates are instrument outputs. One judge; JBS audits stance, not IAS scoring. Temperature zero sets sampling variance aside. Nine snapshots; the PCA is exploratory.

Naming this shape, rather than collapsing it to a coordinate, is the precondition for any subsequent conversation about what an LLM’s ideology ought to be.
